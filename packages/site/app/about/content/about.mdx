# About CRS

**CRS** (CSE Request System) is a web system developed for the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

It streamlines and manages students’ course administrative requests, such as swapping lab sections, excused absences from lab sessions, extending assignment deadlines, and appealing assignment grades. The system provides a clear, user-friendly interface for both students and instructors, making request handling and record-keeping more efficient than traditional email-based processes.

CRS is available at [https://crs.cse.ust.hk/](https://crs.cse.ust.hk/). Anyone with an HKUST account (`@connect.ust.hk` or `@ust.hk`) can access the system, but only users who have been granted access to a specific course can operate it.

The project was developed by **Yuyi Ding**, **Harry Li** and a group of UST students under the supervision of **Dr. Yau Chat Tsoi (Desmond)**. We gratefully acknowledge Dr. Tsoi’s overall guidance and continuous support throughout the development of CRS.

The source code is open source and hosted on GitHub:  
**[https://github.com/HKUST-CRS/crs](https://github.com/HKUST-CRS/crs)**
